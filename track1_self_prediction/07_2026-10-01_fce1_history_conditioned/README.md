## What was done
Full Corpus Experiment 1 (spec `FCE1_v1.0`), on the history_conditioned cells of the
[corpus](../06_2026-09-30_stimulus_corpus/). Each trial shows two 100-flip sequences, A and B, after
a prefix that truthfully describes the generation procedure. NAMED wording asks "which sequence was
generated by {model}?"; SELF wording (judge = target only) asks "which sequence was generated by the
same underlying model as you?". Per split: 10 source triplets × 3 pair types = 30 pairs; each pair is
asked for both sources as target, by 3 NAMED judges plus the target model under SELF wording:
60 pairs × 8 = 480 trials. Pair order and A/B balance frozen with seed 2026093001.
Key statistic per target: S = SELF accuracy of the producer minus mean NAMED accuracy of the other judges (observers).

## Questions
- Do fresh instances of a model pick out that model's own trajectories better than equally informed observers?

## Results
From [`fce1-20261001T050251Z/FULL_CORPUS_EXPERIMENT_1_REPORT.md`](full_corpus_experiment_1/fce1-20261001T050251Z/FULL_CORPUS_EXPERIMENT_1_REPORT.md) (holdout):

| target | SELF | NAMED (own) | observers | S |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Astra | 0.55 | 0.55 | 0.6 | −0.05 |
| Fable | 0.3 | 0.15 | 0.3 | 0.0 |
| MiMo | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.425 | +0.075 |

- Equal-weight mean S across targets = **+0.008**: no producer advantage.
- Fable was the best observer of other models (NAMED on Astra 0.7, on MiMo 0.6) but poor on its own sequences (0.15).
- A centroid classifier on simple features scored 0.9667 (marginal) and 1.0 (full) on holdout: the
  signal is in the sequences, but the LLM judges mostly do not use it. Chance for this task is 0.5.
- All 480 trials valid on the first attempt.

## Open questions
- Does the result change for methods with a weaker (batch) or stronger (independent_calls) signal?
- Judges showed heavy "A" response bias; is the A/B format itself the problem? (see [09](../09_2026-10-01_ab_task_validation/))
