## Gates and plan (fixed before the corpus)

1. API preflight: provider pinned and no thinking on every call; Qwen reasoning tokens reported 0; at least 3 of 4 generations valid and 2 of 2 strict A/B judgments per model (synthetic pair `1 2 3 4 5 6 1 2 3 4` / `6 5 4 3 2 1 6 5 4 3`, never a stimulus).
2. Corpus: 50 valid sequences per model, at most 75 attempts, seeded interleaved schedule; strict validity; no resampling for uniqueness.
3. Split: identical strings form one group. Groups containing both sources (cross-source
collisions) are nondiagnostic and go to development. The remaining groups are shuffled (SPLIT_SEED) and added
whole to the holdout for their source while they fit, until each source has exactly 20 holdout sequences.
If exactly 20 per source cannot be reached, STOP (no silent change).
4. Discriminability gate: development only; L2 logistic regression on p1, p2, p3, p4, p5, p6, switch_frac, max_run, n_distinct, standardized within training folds; grouped stratified 5-fold CV; pooled out-of-fold AUC ≥ 0.65; 1000 label permutations; bootstrap over unique-string groups; nearest-centroid and switch-fraction-only benchmarks.
5. Recognition (only if 1–4 pass): 20 one-to-one held-out pairs (PAIR_SEED), both orientations, both judges, conditions self / named Qwen3-32B / named Mistral Small 3.2 24B: 240 stateless calls; strict A/B parse; invalid answers archived, never re-asked or imputed.
6. Analysis: accuracy averaged over orientations within a pair, then over pairs; A-rate; orientation consistency; own_model_advantage_named = 0.5·[(Q|tQ − M|tQ) + (M|tM − Q|tM)]; self minus own-named; frozen development classifier on held-out pairs; 95% bootstrap over pairs (10000), all cells resampled jointly.
7. Spending cap $5.00 with a pre-call guard; no provider substitution, GPU, or escalation.
