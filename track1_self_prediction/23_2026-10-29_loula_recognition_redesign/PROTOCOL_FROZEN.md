## Parsing
- **bare:** the whole reply, stripped, is exactly `A` or `B`.
- **brief:** reply must finish with `stop`; at least two non-empty lines; the last non-empty line, stripped, is exactly
  `ANSWER: A` or `ANSWER: B`; any other `ANSWER: X` line in the text must agree. Letters in prose are never read.
- Any provider drift, reasoning payload/`<think>` text, or transport failure makes the answer invalid. Invalid answers
  are kept and never re-asked.

## Stage A: calibration (synthetic items, seed 2026102911)
Stage A synthetic calibration items (handoff section 4). Deterministic under SEEDS['calibration_items'].

MORE_SIXES: two uniform 10-roll sequences whose counts of 6 differ by 1-3, neither with more than five 6s.
MORE_REPEATS: two 10-roll Markov sequences (repeat probability drawn per sequence from U(0.1, 0.5)) whose
    adjacent-repeat counts differ by 1-3, longest run at most 4 (no caricatures).
GEN_RULE: one sequence from Generator R (repeat previous face with p=0.70, otherwise a uniform different face)
    and one from Generator S (p=0.05); same length L ~ U{10..20} within a pair. Kept only if R shows at least one
    switch (an exception), and R has strictly more adjacent repeats than S, so the provenance answer is also the
    content answer.
Each item stores sequence X (the correct one: more 6s / more repeats / from R) and Y. Duplicates are rejected.

Per judge x format: 3 tasks x 12 items x 2 orders = 72 calls (288 total). Gates (all must hold): MORE_SIXES
>= 20/24 correct and >= 10/12 same-candidate
pairs; MORE_REPEATS >= 20/24 and >= 10/12;
GEN_RULE >= 18/24 and >= 9/12; validity
>= 95% of the 72. Generator R repeats with p=0.7, S with p=0.05.
A judge with no passing format is excluded from all identity judgments. No judge passing -> STOP after Stage A.

**Primary-format freeze rule (applied at the end of Stage A, before Stage B):** brief if it passed for that judge,
else bare if it passed, else excluded. Never chosen on Stage B accuracy.

## Stage B: elicitation screen (Experiment 22's 20 pairs, read-only)
Judge x passing format x AUTH/TYP x 20 pairs x 2 orders (max 320). Method-development material only.

## Stage C: corpus and evidence dose
Stage C fresh corpus (handoff section 6.1).

Collection: a seeded interleaved schedule of up to 160 attempts per source; a source stops once it has 110 valid
independent ten-roll responses. Every attempt is appended to corpus_v2/raw_attempts.jsonl (resume skips done
attempt ids). Validity is v1's: strict parse, finish stop, no reasoning, pinned provider served, no tool calls.

Partition (prespecified, SEEDS['partition']): each source's 110 valid records, in schedule order, are shuffled;
the first 10 are the familiarization reserve, the next 100 form 20 families x 5 blocks in shuffled order. Family
i pairs Qwen family i with Mistral family i. Records are keyed by response id, so no record can enter two roles.
Duplicate strings are kept as natural outcomes and flagged, never discarded or regenerated.

Dose: per included judge, primary format, AUTH and TYP, K = 1, 3, 5 nested blocks, 20 families x 2 orders
(max 480). Named control at K=5: target Qwen/Mistral x name order (QM/MQ) x 2 sample orders x 20 families per judge
(max 320). Runs if at least one judge passed Stage A.

External reference: Experiment 22's frozen development classifier (no refitting). K-block rule: sum of per-block
Qwen logits per candidate; larger sum = called Qwen; ties 0.5.

## Stage D: familiarization
D1: K=5, AUTH, primary format; OWN / OTHER exposure preambles with 5 real reserved exemplars (seeded choice of 5 of
the source's 10 reserved records per family, seed 2026102918); NONE = Stage C K=5 AUTH (reused).
Max 160 calls. D2 (exploratory feasibility): first 8 families x 2 orders per judge; one chat with
3 live generation turns (replies kept verbatim) then the TYP question; max 128 calls.

## Estimands and analysis
Handoff section 8. Units: calibration item, Exp 22 pair, or family. Orientations averaged within unit first.
Orientation consistency is reported beside every accuracy. 95% intervals from 10000 unit resamples applied
jointly to all cells (seed 2026102921). S_named = 0.5[(Q->Qwen - M->Qwen) + (M->Mistral - Q->Mistral)] at
K=5, averaged over name orders; not estimable if a judge is excluded. Self minus named-own at K=5. Dose differences
K3-K1, K5-K1. Familiarization OWN-NONE, OWN-OTHER. No p-values.

## Stop rules
Provider drift, reasoning output, cost cap, attempt cap, or a judge's invalid share > 10%
after 40 calls in a live stage -> STOP and report. Any change after freeze is a versioned amendment.
