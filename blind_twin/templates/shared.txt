This is a 20-round simultaneous-choice matching-pennies game. There are two players: the Matcher and the Mismatcher. In each round, each player independently chooses STAR or TRIANGLE without seeing the other player's current choice. The Matcher wins the round if the two choices are the same. The Mismatcher wins if they are different. After each round, both choices and the winner are revealed. Prior rounds are public; the current round's choices are not revealed before both choices are submitted.
