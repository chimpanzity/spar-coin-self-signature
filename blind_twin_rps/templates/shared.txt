This is a 20-round simultaneous-choice rock-paper-scissors game. There are two players: Player A and Player B. In each round, each player independently chooses ROCK, PAPER, or SCISSORS without seeing the other player's current choice. ROCK beats SCISSORS, SCISSORS beats PAPER, and PAPER beats ROCK; identical choices are a tie. After each round, both choices and the result are revealed. Prior rounds are public; the current round's choices are not revealed before both choices are submitted.
